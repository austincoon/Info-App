Misc Notes

  • New provider: NPI # goes in the comments section. Medicaid # goes in the e-mail section.
  • Wheelchair policy: Attendant or client. (Cannot be both)
  • VA: We cannot transport clients to VA facilities
  • Attendant policies: Must be over 18. Only 1 attendant allowed (Exceptions allowed for both parents traveling with child)
  • ER: We cannot transport clients to or from Emergency Room.
  • Nursing Homes: We do not provide transportation for nursing homes.
  • RX: Pharmacy needs to be in home county or on route to appointment (Possible exceptions)
